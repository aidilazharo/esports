About the Programme

STEM, TVET, AI, Simulation, Digital Innovation & Esports Talent Development Programme

A school-based talent development programme designed to give students practical exposure to digital technology, artificial intelligence, innovation, simulation, STEM, TVET and electronic sports through structured learning, activities and competitions.

Programme Objectives

What the programme aims to develop

The programme focuses on practical exposure, student participation and long-term digital talent development.

Digital Innovation

Encourage students to explore ideas, problem-solving, prototyping and innovation using digital technology.

Artificial Intelligence

Introduce students to AI concepts, responsible use of AI tools and practical AI-assisted learning activities.

STEM & TVET Skills

Build interest in applied science, technology, engineering and technical-vocational pathways through hands-on activities.

Esports Development

Use structured esports activities to develop teamwork, communication, discipline, strategy and digital participation.

Simulation & Applied Learning

Expose students to simulation-based learning and technology-assisted practice in a safe and structured environment.

Talent & Leadership

Create opportunities for students, teachers and schools to participate in activities, events and inter-school collaboration.

How It Works

Student participation journey

1

School Registration

The school registers in the portal and appoints a school team teacher as the main coordinator.

2

Student Membership

Students register under their school and pay a one-time RM100 participation fee.

3

Learning & Attendance

Active students can participate in weekly learning modules, school activities and attendance-based programme sessions.

4

Events & Competitions

Students and schools can join eligible events, competitions and tournaments through the programme portal.

Membership

RM100

The student participation fee is RM100, payable one time only.

  • RM100 one-time participation fee
  • Access to programme learning modules
  • Attendance and participation tracking
  • Eligibility to join selected events and competitions
  • Student STEM & Esports certificate access
Learning Modules

Structured weekly digital learning

Active students can follow structured learning modules that combine digital knowledge, practical activities, responsible technology use and competition-related skills.

Digital Literacy & Responsible Technology

Digital citizenship, online safety, responsible technology use and basic digital productivity.

Artificial Intelligence Fundamentals

Introduction to AI, prompting, AI-assisted learning, responsible AI use and practical school-level applications.

Coding & Computational Thinking

Problem-solving, logic, basic coding concepts and computational thinking through guided activities.

STEM, TVET & Simulation

Applied STEM, technical concepts, simulation-based learning and exposure to TVET pathways.

Innovation & Project Development

Idea generation, problem definition, prototyping, teamwork and presentation of student projects.

Esports Skills & Performance

Game strategy, teamwork, sportsmanship, tournament preparation, gameplay review and performance improvement.

School Development Support

Equipment eligibility

Participating schools receive programme equipment in stages as student participation grows, with PlayStation 5 and account activation released after the active-participant requirement is achieved.

Equipment Eligibility

10 registered participants

Programme equipment is released in stages based on student participation recorded in the portal.

After 10 participants:
Programme equipment and accessories will be sent to the school.
After 30 active participants:
The PlayStation 5 and account activation details will be sent to the school.
High-end workstation:
Schools receive 1 workstation for every 100 active students for STEM, AI, simulation and digital innovation activities.
School Reference Eligibility

Grow the programme network

Reference eligibility requires:

  • The referring school has at least 5 registered students.
  • At least 2 schools use it as their referrer.
  • At least 2 referred schools each have 30 active students.
1 set PlayStation 5
1 console, 2 controllers, 1 PS Camera and 43-inch digital TV.

Teacher STEM & Esports Certificate

Question Answer
What is the programme?

The programme is a school-based STEM, TVET, AI, simulation, digital innovation and esports talent development initiative combining structured learning, activities, attendance, certificates and competitions.
How much is the student participation fee?

The student participation fee is RM100 one time only.
When will the school receive equipment?

After the school reaches 10 registered participants, programme equipment and accessories will be sent to the school.
When will the school receive the PlayStation 5 and account activation?

The PlayStation 5 and account activation details will be sent only after the school reaches 30 active participants.
How do students access their certificates?

Eligible students and teachers can sign in through the programme login page and access available certificates from their portal.
What learning and activities are included?

The programme includes structured learning in digital literacy, AI, coding, STEM, TVET, simulation, innovation and esports, together with attendance tracking, events and competitions.
Can students join tournaments?

Yes. Eligible students can participate in programme competitions and tournaments published through the portal.
Who coordinates the programme at school?

Each participating school registers a School Team Teacher who coordinates programme participation, student learning and related activities.
